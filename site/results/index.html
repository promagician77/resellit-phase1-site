Not measured

  • The live Poshmark form. No account was used and nothing was listed; field labels get checked on your account in week 1.
  • Poshmark’s response to repeated automated fills on one account. Every publish in this design is a manual click.
  • Your ReSellit app and your existing extension: not seen yet. The audit script and connector are ready for them.
  • Edge and Brave (both Chromium). Only Chromium/Chrome was run.
  • Photos larger than 2 MB each. The test photos are 15–40 KB.
